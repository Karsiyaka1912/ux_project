# Step 2 · Benchmark

Main task I tested: find out which event my group goes to tonight and who is drinking
Device and width: iPhone 390 px

|                | A: WhatsApp group poll | B: Partiful | C: Open Invite (Social Calendar) |
|----------------|------------------------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Taps to goal   | 10 to 13 | 6 to 8 | 7 to 9 |
| Works 1        | everyone votes with one tap and results update live | after you RSVP you see who else is going | "Who's Down": post a quick idea and see which friends are interested |
| Works 2        | it lives in the chat the group already uses; you can see who voted, or hide names | guests can join from a link, no app needed | a "Friends" filter shows the events your friends are attending |
| Works 3        | you can set an end time, and pin or filter polls so they do not get lost | guests can RSVP to several options and the host picks one | ideas expire after 24 hours, so the list stays fresh |
| Fails 1        | one question per poll: "which event" and "who drinks" need two polls | built around one host and one event, not a group choosing between several events tonight | built for friends and plans in general, not for club nights or drink counts |
| Fails 2        | polls get buried in a busy chat | some users are annoyed when they must download the app to see an invite | everyone has to have the app and be friends in it |
| Fails 3        | up to 12 options, no photos, no explanation per option | some users feel it lacks features and complain about frequent updates | a young app, so little user feedback exists |
| Review quote 1 | "Polls get buried in active group chats within minutes, so nobody even sees them and we still have to text everyone individually." | "Everyone has to sign up with their phone number just to RSVP, which kills the vibe when you are trying to make a quick last-minute plan." | no reviews yet |
| Review quote 2 | "Trying to get a clear headcount in a 15-person chat is impossible because half the group replies to old messages and the rest ignore it." | "Great for big parties weeks in advance, but way too many steps and extra questions just to figure out who is coming out tonight." | no reviews yet |

Patterns I will reuse: a live tally everyone can see, names next to answers, one-tap answers, ideas that expire.

Gaps nobody solves: nobody puts "which event" and "who drinks" on the same screen, and nobody turns the answers into a number the group can use for a shared drinks purchase.
