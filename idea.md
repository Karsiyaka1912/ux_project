# Step 1 · Idea

Working title: Pop Out!

## The idea in one sentence
Pop Out! helps a large friend group (10 to 20 people) who go to rave and techno events together to see who wants to go to which event and who will drink, on the day they decide where to go.

## Who it is for
- User group: friends in Budapest who go to rave and techno events together, in a group of 10 to 20
- Where I can find five of them: my own friend group (4 have already agreed to talk to me)
- Device and situation: iPhone, in the days and hours before an event, often away from a desk

## The problem (my hypothesis)
I believe deciding where to go as a group takes too much effort, because everyone's preferences are collected by calling or talking to people one by one.
I also believe the alcohol question matters because the group buys drinks together, which is cheaper, so someone has to know how many people will drink before the night.
I will know I am right if, in interviews, people tell me about repeating the same question to many people, or about not knowing who is coming until the last minute, or about a shared purchase that was planned with a wrong headcount.

## Platform and why
iOS (iPhone app), because it is used on the phone, around the time of the event.

## Out of scope
1. buying tickets
2. chat or messaging
3. automatic discovery of all events in the city (the prototype uses a short list of example events)
